# How to play PawFall

You are **SuperColby**, an orange tabby in a blue C-shield shirt and an orange cape. Perring Parkway Pete, a feral red-eyed rat in a tactical vest, has stolen 32 treasures and scattered them across a 120-screen jungle. Get them back.

## Controls

| Action | Keyboard | Gamepad / touch |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Run left / right | `←` `→` (or `A` `D`) | d-pad / stick, touch d-pad |
| Climb ladders and vines | `↑` `↓` (or `W` `S`) | d-pad up / down |
| Jump | `Space`, `Z` or `X` | A button, touch jump button |
| Pause | `P` or `Esc` | |
| Mute / CRT filter / Fullscreen | `M` / `C` / `F` | |

## Rules

- You start with **2000 points** and **3 lives**.
- The clock counts down from **20:00**. Running out of time costs a life.
- There are **32 treasures** (tuna tins, silver sardines, gold bars, diamond collars). Each one adds points.
- Falling into a hole, tar, water or a croc, or touching fire, a cobra or a scorpion, costs a life.
- Rolling logs and cheese wheels knock you back. Jump them.
- Walking underground moves you **3 screens** per tunnel step, as in the original Pitfall!.
- Pete ambushes you from the canopy on his screens. His billboards mark them.

## Tips

- Jump early over water and tar; the timing is generous, but the landing is not.
- Vines swing in an arc. Jump at the bottom of the swing to reach the far side.
- The sky changes from day to golden hour to dusk to night as the clock runs down. Night screens are harder to read, so slow down.
- Tunnels are the fastest way across the jungle, but you give up the surface treasures on the way.
